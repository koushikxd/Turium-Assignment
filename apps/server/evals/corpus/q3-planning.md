Q3 planning meeting, product team

Attendees: Amara, Jonas, Li Wei, Priya, Sam. Notes by Sam.

Where we are. Q2 closed with the new onboarding flow shipped, but activation only moved from 31 to 34 percent against a target of 40. Jonas thinks the drop-off is at the calendar connection step, which asks for broad permissions before the user has seen any value. The funnel data agrees: 38 percent of people who reach that step leave.

Decisions. First, we will make calendar connection optional during onboarding and ask for it later, when the user first tries to schedule something. Jonas leads it and expects a first version in three weeks. Second, the mobile app rewrite is paused for the whole of Q3. It was eating two engineers and had no user-facing milestone before November. Amara was against pausing it but agreed to revisit at the Q3 review rather than argue now. Third, Li Wei moves from mobile to the integrations squad to help with the Slack connector, which three of our five largest customers asked for.

Not decided. Whether to raise prices for new customers in Q3. Priya wants to wait for the activation fix so we are not changing two things at once; Sam wants to test it on a small segment now. We agreed to decide at the next meeting with the numbers from finance.

Targets for Q3. Activation at 40 percent by end of September. Slack connector in beta with at least two of the customers who asked for it. Weekly support tickets about calendar permissions down by half.

Next meeting in two weeks, same time. Sam to circulate the funnel dashboard link before then.
