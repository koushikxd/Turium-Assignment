Lisbon trip, 9 to 14 May

Flights. Out on TP1351, leaving at 07:40 from terminal 1, landing 10:15 local time. Back on TP1356 on the 14th at 18:25. Seats 14A and 14B both ways. Only cabin bags, so no waiting at the belt.

Stay. Apartment in Alfama, five nights, booked directly with the host, Rita. Check-in is from 15:00; she will leave the keys in a lockbox by the door and send the code by message on the morning we arrive. The street is steep and cobbled, so no suitcases with small wheels. Early arrival means we have five hours to fill with bags, so the plan is to leave them at the luggage storage place near Santa Apolónia station and walk along the river.

Getting around. Buy Viva Viagem cards at the airport metro station and load them with zapping credit; it works on the metro, trams and the ferry to Cacilhas. Tram 28 is always packed with tourists; catch it early in the morning from Martim Moniz, or just walk.

Food list. Pastéis de nata at Manteigaria, eaten warm. Grilled sardines anywhere with a charcoal grill outside. Bifana at a counter. Dinner on Saturday is booked at 20:30 at a small place in Graça that Rita recommended; the booking is under my surname.

Day trip. Sintra on Tuesday by train from Rossio, about 40 minutes. Book the Pena Palace tickets online for the first entry slot, because the queues later in the day are long. Wear proper shoes.

Budget. Roughly 120 euros a day for the two of us, not counting the apartment, which is already paid.
