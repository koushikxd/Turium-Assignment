Car maintenance log: 2019 Honda Jazz

Oil: fully synthetic 0W-20, 3.4 litres with a filter change. The manual says every 10,000 km or once a year, whichever comes first. Last change at 84,200 km in February, at the garage on Mill Road. Next change due at 94,200 km or February next year.

Tyres: 185/55 R16 front and back. Summer tyres are on now; the winter set is stored at my parents' garage, labelled by position with chalk. Pressure is 2.3 bar front and 2.2 bar rear when cold. Rotated front to back at the last oil change; rotate again at the next one. Tread on the fronts was at 4 mm in February, so plan to replace them before winter.

Brakes: front pads replaced at 71,000 km. The mechanic said the rear shoes still have more than half left. Brake fluid was changed two years ago and should be done every three years, so it is due next spring.

Battery: original battery replaced in November after the car would not start on a cold morning. The new one is a 45 Ah EFB battery with a three-year warranty; the receipt is in the glovebox folder.

Other: the cabin air filter was changed in February and made a big difference to the smell when the fan runs. The wiper blades squeak on the windscreen when it is only drizzling; try cleaning the glass with isopropyl alcohol before buying new ones. The insurance renewal is on 3 September and the inspection certificate expires on 12 October.
